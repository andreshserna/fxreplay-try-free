# Copy and claims

This is a candidate demo that carries someone else's brand.

- **No source, no claim.** Every factual statement (features, plan limits, prices, markets,
  numbers) traces to a page on fxreplay.com recorded in `docs/research/brief.md` with its URL
  and the date it was read.
- No invented testimonials, ratings, user counts, logos or press. Social proof appears only
  when it is real and sourced.
- No promise or implication of profit. Backtesting is practice, not a result.
- The offer is the free plan: no card, no time limit, its limits stated plainly. Do not blur
  it with the trial of the paid plans.
- The headline and the offer wording are the candidate's decisions. Propose options. Do not
  settle them.
- The page says, discreetly, that it is a candidate challenge demo and not the official
  FX Replay site.
- Indexing is off by default behind an environment flag. SEO is fully implemented and is
  audited with the flag on.
- A section earns its place by answering an objection that stands between the visitor and the
  signup. If it answers none, cut it.
- Do not fake product screenshots. Use real imagery sourced cleanly from fxreplay.com, or an
  original illustration in SVG and CSS.
- The product is in English. Design for phones first: paid social and affiliate traffic is
  assumed to land there.
